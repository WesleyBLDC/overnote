An “Actions” token replaces the notes view. The short list from the earlier option B is on the left, and the right pane shows the highlighted submenu instead of a note. Backspace on the token goes back to notes.

Size
700 × up to 520 pt, centred, 15% from the top of the screen. 18 pt corners, surface rgba(38,38,42,0.80) with 30 pt blur. Field 56 pt, 21 pt type.
Key actions
Five fixed chips: New, Pin, Copy link, Terminal, Settings. 30 pt tall, 9 pt corners. They hide while typing and return when the field is cleared.
Keys
⌘P opens on notes, ⌘K on actions, “>” switches to actions. ↑↓ move, ↩ open, → opens a submenu, ⌘⌫ deletes the highlighted note.
Sort
“Modified ▾” menu: Last opened, Modified, Created, Title, Length; Newest first; Pinned on top. The meta line under each note follows the sort.
