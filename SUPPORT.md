# Getting help

The docs answer most questions, and they are the fastest route.

- **Installing, updating, backups, or a panel that will not open:** [`docs/install.md`](docs/install.md), including its Troubleshooting section.
- **Markdown and writing tips:** [`docs/writing-notes.md`](docs/writing-notes.md).
- **Shortcuts and commands:** the Usage section of the [README](README.md#usage), or ⌘K in the panel.
- **Scripting Overnote or driving it from an agent:** [overnote-axi](https://github.com/WesleyBLDC/overnote-axi) and [`docs/architecture.md`](docs/architecture.md).

Still stuck, found a bug, or have an idea?
[Open an issue](https://github.com/WesleyBLDC/overnote/issues/new/choose) and pick the form that fits.
Search the open issues first; a thumbs up on an existing one helps.

Report security problems privately, as [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) describes, never in a public issue.
